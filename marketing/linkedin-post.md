## Longer version (if you want to explain the build)

I've been building a jungle survival game in the browser, and this is the first look.

**The story:** Flight 7 loses its engine and crashes on an uncharted island. You're the only survivor.

**The loop:** find the supply crates, locate fresh water, gather sticks and stone, craft a campfire, hunt, cook, sleep. Every system pushes on the others: dirty water makes you sick, raw meat is a gamble, and night brings jaguars.

**Under the hood:**
- Three.js and Vite, no game engine
- The island, trees, textures and even the sounds are generated in code at start-up
- Day and night cycle with image-based lighting, bloom and colour grading
- Dynamic resolution so it holds its frame rate on weaker machines

**Honest status:** it's a prototype. The character is still a placeholder I'm replacing, and the animals and balance need work. I'll post updates as it grows.

If you've shipped a browser game, I'd love to hear what you'd prioritise next.
